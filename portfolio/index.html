About Me

Hey! I’m Lalit—a Computer Science student from Bhopal who enjoys building useful things for the web.

I spend most of my time turning rough ideas into clean, working websites. Instead of just making practice clones, I like building stuff people around me can actually use—like Bhukkad for local food and tiffins, or an online store setup for Manya Medical Store.

When I’m not coding, I’m usually hosting college events, managing fests, or exploring new ideas. Always up for learning and building cool projects.
